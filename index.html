Common Questions

Before calling, you might find answers to your questions here:

How do I reset my password?

Visit the login page and click "Forgot Password" to receive reset instructions via email.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Account > Subscription > Cancel and follow the instructions.

Why isn't my Windows updating?

Check your internet connection, available storage, or try running the Windows Update troubleshooter.
